Align Pro

Align every mic, and hear exactly how much.

    One Millisecond Is All It Takes

    At 48 kHz, one millisecond is 48 samples. A one millisecond offset between two mics cancels the frequencies around 500 Hz and its odd harmonics. That is why a kick sounds hollow, a miked guitar cab blended with a DI loses its body, and a choir array feels thin the moment everything is summed. It happens long before anyone reaches for an EQ.

    Align Pro fixes the timing and the phase between every mic at once. And because a little disagreement between mics is part of what makes a room sound like a room, it lets you choose how much to fix.

    Where It Matters

    Drums, Cabs and Choir Arrays

    Any source picked up by more than one mic: the kick in and out, snare top and bottom, overheads and room, a guitar cab with its DI, or a choir recorded with a spaced pair and spot mics.

    Insert, Play Ten Seconds, Align

    Insert Align Pro on every mic of the source. Play about ten seconds so the analysis can find each arrival, then press Align All. Every group aligns to its own reference and the correlation figures update. With ARA2 there is nothing to play at all, because your DAW hands over the audio and the analysis happens without a capture pass.

    Every Mic Against Every Other Mic

    Align Pro does not just compare each track with its own reference. It scores the whole array at once in a correlation matrix, which is where you find the pair that is fighting, like the overhead and the room that never agreed, or the snare bottom pulling against the kick.

    A Dial for How Far to Take It

    Alignment at full strength can flatten a kit. The Amount dial scales how much of the correction is applied, live, from none of it to all of it. You hear the correlation climb and stop at the point where the problem is fixed but it still sounds like a drum kit.

    Questions

    What does the Amount dial actually do?
    It scales how much of the calculated correction is applied, from none to all of it, while you listen. Full correction is not always what a kit wants, because some of what makes a room sound like a room is the mics disagreeing.
    Do I have to play the session through first?
    About ten seconds, so the analysis can find each arrival. Under ARA2 your DAW hands over the audio and there is no capture pass at all.
    Does it work in Pro Tools?
    Not in version 1. AAX is not built, and it is not claimed.
    What about Logic?
    The AU builds and loads, but it has not yet been run inside an actual Logic session, so this page does not claim that it works there.
    Is there a dongle?
    No. A key you type into the plug-in, two computers at a time, and a Deactivate button when you move.
