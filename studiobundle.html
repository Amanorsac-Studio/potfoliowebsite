A Studio Mix, Set Up in Your Church

Most church setups start from an empty session every week, or from whatever the last person left behind. The Studio Bundle starts you from the session the studio actually uses: tracks named, buses routed, the chain already on every channel.

You pick the template that fits, mixing or live stream, and the four plug-ins come with either one. Then we get on a call and put it on your computer, on your interface, at your levels, so the first Sunday you use it is not the day you find out how it works.
