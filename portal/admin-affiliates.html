Approving makes their code: a word they will say out loud, 4 to 16 letters or digits, upper-cased. The buyer's discount and their cut are set here per person and default to 10% and 20%. An approval emails them the code, the link and the rules; a decline is quiet.

Declined

Owed is what the next payout would be: sales past the 14-day hold, less anything paid and then refunded. Switching someone off also stops their code the same minute; what they are owed stays owed. Open a row to see every sale with the buyer's email, and to reverse one after a refund that did not come through Stripe (Paystack refunds are by hand).

The monthly run. Pay each line from PayPal or MoMo yourself, then record it here with the transaction reference: that marks the earnings paid and emails the affiliate. Nothing under $25 is listed, and each currency is its own line.
