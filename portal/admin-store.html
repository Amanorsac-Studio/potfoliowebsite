    How the store works

    A creator applies on /store/creators. You approve them here and they get a dashboard at /store/dashboard, where New song opens the five-step upload: the song, the cover, the ten tracks as WAV, the sections, the price. Submit lands it in Review. You listen, finish the sections, and approve; it goes on the shelf and PerformLive can install it. The catalogue rows marked coming soon are the seventy artists' releases, waiting for their stems.

    Approving makes their creator page at the address you give and opens their dashboard. Declining sends your line back to them.

    Load the guide and a couple of stems and listen. The sections are yours to finish: tap a name while the track plays, or type the seconds, then save. Approve puts it on the shelf; send back returns it with your note.
