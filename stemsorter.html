Every session starts the same way.

Forty files land in a folder: KICK #03, OVH L #03, TNR 2 #03, SNARE DWN #03. You open your template and drag them in one by one, find the right track, fix the levels. Twenty minutes later you can finally press play. Now do that every Sunday, for every client, after every rehearsal.

Names

It understands names.

OH, OVH, Overheads and Over L are all overheads. LV, Lead Vox and WL are your lead vocal. TNR 2 is the second tenor mic. Stem Sorter already knows hundreds of these, and you can teach it your engineers' names in seconds.

Template

It knows your template.

Point it at your template. It reads every track and folder and matches each incoming channel to the right one: left and right, 1, 2 and 3. The few channels it isn't sure about go to the top of the list in amber, so you check three rows, not forty.

Build

It hands you a session.

Press Build and get a session for your DAW with every file in place. Studio One and REAPER open ready to play. Logic, Pro Tools, Cubase and Digital Performer get an AAF with your tracks named and in order. Your inserts, sends and buses stay exactly as you saved them.

Set up your template once.

Stem Sorter only places files on tracks that already exist. That is what keeps your sessions safe, so give it room:

  • Keep a few spare tracks in each section: SPARE 1–SPARE 4 under BGVs, a couple under Keys, one or two under Drums. Stem Sorter uses tracks named SPARE, EXTRA, FREE, OPEN or TBD when a channel has nowhere else to go.
  • Name tracks the way files arrive: OH L, SNARE BOTTOM, TNR 2, CRWD R. The closer the names, the fewer rows to check.

Questions, answered.

Will it change my original recordings?
No. When it normalizes, it works on copies (or, in REAPER, on item gain you can undo).
Do I need to be online?
Only to activate. After that it works offline and checks your licence quietly when it can.
Can I use it on my laptop and my studio computer?
Yes, one key covers two computers. You can free a seat from the About screen or from My Apps at amanorsac.studio.
My engineer names things differently.
Open Edit name aliases, add their names, and save. Stem Sorter learns them for good, and you can share the file with other producers.
Why doesn't it create missing tracks?
Creating tracks means rewriting parts of your session that can't be guaranteed safe across DAW versions. Spare tracks in your template give you the same result with no risk.
What if a file doesn't match any track?
It appears at the top in amber. Assign it to any track in a click, skip it, or add a spare track to your template so it has a home next time.
Is it signed?
Yes. The Windows installer is Authenticode-signed, and the macOS app is Developer ID-signed and notarized by Apple.
