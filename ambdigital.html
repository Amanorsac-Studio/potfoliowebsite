    PRISM EQ

    Precision Equalizer

    Twenty-four bands, and every one can live in the middle, the sides, the left or the right.

    A precision equaliser you work by hand. The curve and a coloured shape for each band sit over the live spectrum, input and output together. Drag a node to move a band, use the wheel for Q, double-click to add or remove one.

    • Up to 24 bands with eight shapes: bell, low and high shelf, low and high cut, notch, band pass and tilt
    • Per-band Stereo, Left, Right, Mid or Side placement, shown as a badge on the node
    • Dynamic bands, downward or upward, with the curve moving live as they work; four colour modes from clean to tape

    12 presets · Vocals · Drums · Bass · Guitars · Keys · Mix Bus · Master · Utility

    FLUX DYNAMIC EQ

    Adaptive Dynamic Equalizer

    An equaliser that only acts when the music needs it.

    Up to twelve bands that sit flat until the signal crosses their threshold, then cut or lift by as much as you allow. Adaptive mode follows the programme level so a setting keeps working as the performance changes.

    • Up to 12 dynamic bands, cutting or boosting, each with its own threshold, range, ratio, attack and release
    • Adaptive thresholds, auto learn, external sidechain, per-band listen and a delta monitor
    • Per-band Stereo, Left, Right, Mid or Side placement

    12 presets · Vocals · Drums · Bass · Guitars · Keys · Mix Bus · Master · Creative · Utility

    SPECTRA MULTIBAND

    Six-Band Dynamics

    Split the mix where you want, and watch every band work.

    Two to six bands of dynamics over the live spectrum. Drag the crossover lines to set the split, drag a band's line to set its threshold, and watch a bar in each band show its gain change as the music plays.

    • 2 to 6 bands with draggable crossovers and thresholds, minimum or linear-phase split
    • Compress, expand up or expand down per band, with ratio, range, attack, release and makeup
    • Per-band solo, bypass and delta, a global link control and a wet/dry mix

    12 presets · Vocals · Drums · Bass · Guitars · Keys · Mix Bus · Master · Creative · Utility

    Screenshot coming — the plug-in is built, the picture is not in the repository yet.

    PULSE LIMITER

    True-Peak Limiter

    See exactly how hard you are hitting it.

    A limiter with a scrolling history: input in grey, output in blue, gain reduction in red from the top. The ceiling and threshold are lines you drag. Four styles, from transparent to dense, and a transient control that decides how much punch survives.

    • Draggable threshold and true-peak ceiling over a live input, output and gain reduction history
    • Four styles, adaptive or manual release, and lookahead reported to the host
    • Transient preserve, stereo link and a delta monitor to hear what is being removed

    12 presets · Vocals · Drums · Bass · Mix Bus · Master · Creative · Utility

    AIR DE-ESSER

    Intelligent De-Essing

    Point at the ess. Watch it go.

    The focus band sits over the live spectrum: drag it to the sibilance, use the wheel to widen or narrow it, and grab the threshold line to set where it starts working. A curve shows the reduction actually being applied, measured from the audio, with the amount in dB.

    • Draggable focus, bandwidth and threshold on the spectrum, with measured reduction drawn live
    • Wide, split and smart modes, and neutral, bright or dark voice profiles
    • Range, attack, release and sensitivity, stereo link, and a listen switch for the detector

    12 presets · Vocals · Drums · Guitars · Mix Bus · Utility

    Screenshot coming — the plug-in is built, the picture is not in the repository yet.

    RESONANCE CTRL

    Spectral Resonance Controller

    Takes the ring out and leaves the tone alone.

    Tames resonances as they appear, inside a range you set by dragging its edges on the spectrum. The display draws the change it is making, so you can see that it is working on the honk and not the body.

    • Draggable working range over the live spectrum, with the measured change drawn in real time
    • Depth, selectivity, speed, sensitivity and smoothing, plus a control to keep transients
    • Learn, freeze and delta, and stereo, mid, side, left or right operation

    12 presets · Vocals · Drums · Guitars · Keys · Mix Bus · Master · Creative · Utility

    FREQ SHAPER

    Harmonic Spectral Shaper

    Add harmonics exactly where the track is missing them.

    Six zones across the spectrum, each with its own harmonics, density and width. Drag a zone up for more, drag the lines between zones to move the splits. Put weight into a bass without touching the top, or air onto a vocal without touching the chest.

    • Six zones with draggable crossovers, each with harmonics, density, width, solo and its own on switch
    • A punch control, direct level and wet/dry mix, with auto gain
    • The measured change drawn over the live spectrum

    12 presets · Vocals · Drums · Bass · Guitars · Keys · Mix Bus · Master · Creative · Utility

    ORBIT DELAY

    Multi-Tap Creative Delay

    A delay you can see travelling.

    A main echo with feedback, plus up to eight taps you place by hand: drag a tap sideways for time and up and down for level. Each tap has its own pan and filter. Changing the time while it plays bends the pitch like a tape echo instead of clicking.

    • A feedback echo plus 8 taps, each with time, level, pan and filter, placed by dragging
    • Sync to host tempo, smooth modulation, and ducking that clears the echoes while you play
    • Freeze to hold the loop, with pitch-bending time changes and no clicks

    12 presets · Vocals · Drums · Bass · Guitars · Keys · Creative

    Screenshot coming — the plug-in is built, the picture is not in the repository yet.

    SPACEVERB PRO

    Algorithmic Reverb

    Set two seconds. Get two seconds.

    A true stereo reverb: early reflections and diffusion feeding an eight-line network, so tails are dense rather than metallic and a mono source comes back wide. The decay control is the real time the tail takes to fall 60 dB, from a tight room to forty seconds.

    • True stereo with early reflections, pre-delay to 500 ms and decay from 0.1 to 40 seconds
    • Size, density, diffusion, early/late balance, and separate low and high damping
    • Smooth modulation, ducking, width to 200 % and a freeze that holds the space

    12 presets · Vocals · Drums · Guitars · Keys · Strings & Horns · Mix Bus · Creative

    WIDE IMAGER

    Frequency-Dependent Stereo

    Wide where it helps. Mono where it matters.

    Five bands of stereo width with a bass-mono control underneath, so the top can open out while the low end stays solid. A vectorscope and a correlation meter show what you are doing to the image, and a safe-width switch and a mono check keep it honest.

    • Five width bands from 0 to 250 % with draggable crossovers, plus a bass-mono frequency
    • Rotation, mid/side balance and focus
    • Live vectorscope and correlation meter, safe width and a one-click mono check
