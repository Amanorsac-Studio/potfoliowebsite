    Shimmer

    A Shimmer in the Path, Not Beside It

    Most shimmer plug-ins put the effect alongside the sound, so the pad and the shimmer arrive as two separate things sitting next to each other. Nebula Tide puts the shimmer in the path. The pad goes in and the shimmer comes out, so what you hear is one sound that grows, rather than two sounds stacked on top of each other.

    One control does the work. At the bottom of its travel it adds a hint of air, and at the top it opens into a room with no ceiling. Turning it moves five things at once, each on its own curve: how much, how close, how bright, how dense and how far it regenerates. The pitch shifting is splice aligned rather than fixed interval, which is the difference between a shimmer that sounds smooth and one that sounds grainy.

    Every Control, If You Want Them

    A full set of shimmer controls waits in Settings for anyone who wants to shape it further: bloom, tone, size, density and pitch. Nobody has to touch them, and the single control on the stage sounds right on its own.

    Studio

    Your Recordings, Our Engine

    The new Studio page has twelve slots, one for each key. Drop your own recordings onto the key you recorded them in and the slot fills. Name it, save it and give it a colour, and it appears on the stage beside the presets we made. There is no folder structure to learn, no manifest to write and no restart. When you share a preset with someone, they import it in one click.

    Questions

    Does my version 1 licence still work?
    Version 1 needs no licence key, and if you had it before 11 October 2026 it stays yours. Version 2 is a separate, paid release with its own key, and installing it leaves version 1 alone.
    Do my presets carry over?
    The twelve key recordings are the same library, so everything you know still sounds like itself. The Studio page is new in version 2, so presets you build there belong to version 2.
    Where do the sounds install?
    Wherever you choose. The installer shows the path before it writes anything, and Settings lets you move the library to another drive later without reinstalling.
    How many machines?
    Two at a time. You can free a slot from inside the app or from My Apps when you change computers.
    Do I need the internet?
    Only to activate the first time, and for a quiet check now and then. After that it works offline for a month at a stretch.
    Is it a plug-in?
    Both. It runs as a standalone app and as a plug-in (VST3, and AU on the Mac), so it sits in a session or beside one. The drag handle puts a MIDI clip on your timeline either way.
