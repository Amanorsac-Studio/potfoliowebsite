Affiliate programme

Send people to the apps. Keep a fifth of every sale.

If you teach keys, lead a worship team, review software or just have people who ask you what you use: your own code and link, 10% off for them, 20% for you, paid monthly.

30 daysof memory. Your link is remembered for a month, so a sale next week still counts.

What the application needs

One video of an existing product. Pick any app or plug-in in the store, show it the way you would to your audience, and send the link. It does not have to be polished and it does not have to be long. It is how the studio sees that you have used the thing and how you talk about it, and it is the one piece of the application that cannot be skipped. If you are approved, that video is also your first post.

How it works

You get a code that is a word, like NENE10, and a link that carries it: amanorsac.studio/chordlight88?ref=NENE10. Share either. The code is typed in at checkout for anyone who came through the link, and the buyer sees the discount before they pay.

Your dashboard shows clicks, sales and what you are owed. An earning clears fourteen days after the sale, which is the refund window, and the cleared total is paid once a month by PayPal or MTN MoMo once it reaches $25. Sales in cedis through Paystack earn in cedis and are paid in cedis.

The rules, in plain words

  • Say it is an affiliate link. Wherever you share it: "affiliate link, I earn a commission" is enough. The FTC and every platform require it, and so do we.
  • No coupon sites, no ads on our name. The code is for your audience, not for RetailMeNot, and not for a Google ad on "Chordlight 88". Either one ends the account.
  • Your own purchases earn nothing. Your code still works for you as a discount; it just pays no commission.
  • Refunds come back out. A refunded sale is taken off what you are owed, or off the next payout if it was already paid.
  • Say true things. Describe the apps as they are. A page's own words and the studio's videos are yours to use; invented features are not.
  • We can change or end the programme with thirty days' notice; what you have earned to that day is still paid.

The whole agreement is in the terms of service, section 2.8.

Apply

One thing is required: a video you made of one of the studio's existing apps or plug-ins, shown the way you would show it to your audience. Any length, any platform; a link is enough. The studio watches every one and answers by email, usually within a few days.
