Sunday Comes Whether or Not the Altos Learned Their Part

Most choirs learn their parts from a voice note, a YouTube link or a shared Drive folder. A voice note has no isolation, a YouTube link has no parts and a Drive folder has no structure. And nobody in the choir is going to install a DAW.

HarmonieMD is built for exactly that gap. Each singer opens the song and hears their own part, as loud or as slow as they need it. The director builds those parts in a proper studio and can see who has practised before rehearsal starts.

For Singers

A Mixer Any Singer Can Use

Solo your part and lower the rest. Slow the song to 85 percent without changing the pitch, loop the bar you keep missing and add a little reverb so it feels like the room. There is no manual to read. If you can move a volume slider, you can use Choir Mode.

For the Director

A Real Studio for the Music Director

The same song has a second surface. MD Mode is a genuine multitrack editor with soprano 1 and 2, alto 1 and 2, tenor 1 and 2 and the song, with sample accurate overdubs and takes that never overwrite each other.

For the Team

See Who Is Ready Before You Walk In

Setlists publish to the whole team, every song carries its key and tempo, and one tap marks a song as practised. The guessing ends.

How a Week With It Looks

Build the setlist, add the songs, assign leaders and publish it to your team. Upload each harmony, or record them in the built-in studio. Each singer then solos their part, slows it down and loops it. On the day, you can see who practised before rehearsal starts.

Takes That Land in Time

Most apps estimate your computer's audio delay. HarmonieMD measures it by playing test tones and listening back through the mic, the way professional studios calibrate.

Never Lose a Take

Re-record a phrase as many times as you like. Every take is kept, and you can switch back to take two with one click.

Works When the WiFi Does Not

Songs download to the device once. After that, rehearsal does not depend on the church network or any network.

Questions

Do I need musical training to use it?
No. If you can move a volume slider, you can use Choir Mode.
Does my whole team need to pay?
No. One subscription covers your whole team, and members join free with your code.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Songs download to the device once and play without a connection. You only need the internet to get new songs or share recordings.
What do I need to record?
A laptop and any microphone, including the built-in one. Run the one-time sync calibration first and your takes will land in time. Wired headphones work best, because Bluetooth adds a large and changing delay.
Can I use my audio interface?
Yes. Input and output devices can be chosen in the app.
Is my music private?
Yes. Songs are private to your team by default. Only songs you choose to publish appear on Explore.
Which platforms?
Windows desktop today.
