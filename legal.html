1. End user licence agreement

Applies to every Amanorsac Studio app and plugin

The short version. You are buying a licence to use the software, not the software itself. One purchase covers two computers at a time, and you can move it between them yourself. Don't resell it, don't pull it apart, and don't expect it to be perfect.

1.1 What you get

When you buy a product, Amanorsac Studio grants you a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to install and use it. The licence does not expire. Updates released during your included update period are part of it, and a version you already have keeps working after that period ends.

1.2 How many machines

Unless the product page says otherwise, one licence key activates two computers at a time. Both may be yours, or one yours and one a studio machine you work on. You may deactivate a machine from inside the product or from My Apps at any time, which frees the slot immediately.

1.3 What you may not do

  • Share, sell, rent, lend or sub-licence your key, or publish it anywhere.
  • Reverse engineer, decompile or disassemble the software, or defeat its licensing, except where law expressly permits it despite this clause.
  • Redistribute the installers, the sound libraries or any content shipped inside them.
  • Use the software to build a competing product.

Sound libraries are licensed, not sold. You may use the audio a product makes in your own music, commercially, with no further payment and no credit required. You may not extract the underlying recordings and distribute them as sounds.

1.4 Activation and the internet

Licensed products check a key against our server the first time, and quietly now and then afterwards. Between checks they work offline, for at least thirty days at a stretch. We do not require an always-on connection, and we do not use a dongle.

1.5 If the licence ends

The licence ends if you break the terms in 1.3. If that happens, stop using the software and remove it. We may deactivate a key that we can show has been shared publicly or used fraudulently.

1.6 No warranty, and the limit of what we owe you

The software is provided as it is. We test it, we care about it, and we still cannot promise it is free of faults or that it will suit a particular purpose. To the fullest extent the law allows, Amanorsac Studio is not liable for lost work, lost recordings, lost income or any indirect loss, and our total liability for any claim is limited to what you paid for the product.

Keep backups. Do not put an unfamiliar plugin in front of an audience without rehearsing with it first. That is true of all software, not only ours.

1.7 The law that applies

This agreement is governed by the law of the place where Amanorsac Studio is established, and any dispute will be heard by the courts of that place. Nothing here removes rights you have as a consumer that cannot be removed by agreement.

2. Terms of service

Buying, accounts and support

The short version. Make an account, buy a product, get a key in My Apps. If a product genuinely does not work for you, write to us and we will sort it out.

2.1 Your account

You need an account to buy anything, to find your licence keys and to download through Amanorsac Hub. Keep your password to yourself. Tell us if you think someone else has it.

2.2 Payment

Payments are taken by Stripe. We never see or store your card details. Prices are in US dollars and shown before you pay. Where a product is sold as pay what you want, what you pay is what you chose, within the range shown at checkout.

2.3 Refunds

Downloadable software cannot be returned in the way a physical thing can, so we do not offer a no-questions refund window. What we do offer is this: if a product does not work on a system it says it supports, and we cannot fix it for you, we refund it. Write to hello@amanorsac.studio within thirty days of buying, tell us what happened, and you will get a straight answer rather than a form.

If the law where you live gives you a stronger right to a refund, that right applies and this clause does not reduce it.

2.4 Availability

We aim to keep downloads and activation working. We do not promise either is available without interruption, and we may take a product off sale at any time. If a product you have bought is withdrawn, your licence still works and you can still download it from My Apps.

2.5 Support

Support is by email, from a person, at hello@amanorsac.studio. There is no support subscription, and there never will be one.

2.6 Changes

We may change these terms. The date at the top of this page says when they last changed, and a change never applies backwards to a purchase you already made.

3. Privacy policy

What is stored, why, and for how long

The short version. Your email address, what you bought, and an anonymous id per computer so the two-machine limit can work. No tracking of what you make, no selling anything to anyone, ever.

The privacy policy is a document of its own, because it is the one the app stores ask for by address and it needs to answer at that address on its own. It covers what the website records, what each app and plug-in sends and does not send, the four companies involved and what each is allowed to do, how long anything is kept, and how to have all of it deleted.
